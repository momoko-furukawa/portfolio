My Belief

A world where everyone can trust their potential and say,
“I am enough as I am.”

To me, a society where everyone can live with ease is one where each person can believe in their own potential and take a confident step forward.

We all carry inherent strengths. Yet at times, our environments can obscure them. While we cannot rewrite the circumstances we were born into, I firmly believe we can always reshape how we see ourselves and how we choose to live.

That is why I want to help build a world where people can truly feel, “I am enough as I am,” trusting their possibilities and carving their own path.

How I approach

“Ease of living” is built from
both within and without.

An internal dialogue to know, understand, and accept who you are. An external environment to encounter diverse perspectives, broaden horizons, and take on new challenges. Stepping outside a safe harbor, drawing insights from the world, and returning to deepen your self-awareness. Through this continuous cycle, we build an authentic inner core and expand our possibilities.

Internal

Knowing and Navigating Who You Are

Understanding the mechanics of the mind and emotions allows us to look at ourselves with clarity. Discovering what truly moves us, brings us joy, or causes us friction forms the foundation to make choices true to ourselves, rather than living in the perceptions and expectations of others. Cultivating a quiet, grounded conviction: “I am enough as I am.”

External

Expanding Horizons & Psychological Safety to Challenge

Encountering diverse lifestyles and perspectives opens our eyes: “Another way of living is possible; perhaps I can do this too.” Having a supportive space to encourage you, alongside a safe haven to return to, empowers you to move forward. Rather than leaving these moments to chance, I aim to design systems and cultures that bring both expansive horizons and psychological safety to more people.

Profile

Momoko Furukawa

Bicultural Brand & Culture Strategist

Began my career at IBM Japan as a Technical Sales specialist, focusing on enterprise integration and business process optimization.

Driven by a deep curiosity about human thoughts, emotions, and the cultural dynamics of organizations, I expanded into brand strategy. Working closely with executive leadership as a branding consultant, I led diverse initiatives from corporate culture transformation and behavioral guidelines to spatial and brand development.

To bridge the structural logic of systems with the qualitative nuances of human culture, I completed my MBA at Hult International Business School in Boston. Currently based in New York, preparing to step into a new role as an IT consultant.
